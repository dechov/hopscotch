# 9 · The Ladder

Melodies aren't made of notes. They're made of **moves**. Seven words, one syllable each:

**scoot · step · hop · skip · jump · leap · fly**

<Toy id="move-ladder" mode="guided" guide={ladderIntro} />

One word per distance — up or down is shown with your hand. (Secret for later: a leap up
and a jump down land on the same *kind* of note. Same family, opposite doors.)
