# 16 · The Seven-Tone Scale

Time to leave the playground. The full seven-tone scale is the five-tone scale **plus two new notes** —
and those two bring the one move it never had: the **scoot**.

<Toy id="scale-lab" />

Read the chant. The five-tone scale chants *step step hop step hop* — no scoots, no danger.
Load the seven-tone scale: there they are, two scoots. Scoots are where the pull lives —
the note that *aches* to slide Home. Spice and danger in the same little move.

Build your own scale too. Any chant that adds up to a fly (12) is legal. Some are
beautiful. Some are haunted. All are yours.

## The necklace trick

One more secret about scales — they're a **necklace**:

<Toy id="necklace" size="inline" />

Spin the whole necklace and nothing *really* changes — same song, new height.
But walk **Home** to a different bead and you get a whole new world from the very
same notes. Musicians have names for those worlds; you get to have feelings about
them first.
