# 12 · Chant a Song *(regroup)*

Take a song everyone knows and read it the Hopscotch way: **move words** for the pitches,
**longs & shorts** for the time.

<Toy id="chant-board" />

Chant it with the board. Clap it. Then walk to it — literal steps and jumps across the room.
When the moves are in your mouth and feet, they're yours. Try to hear where OTHER songs
stay, step, and jump.
