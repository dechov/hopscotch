# 11 · Make a Sound

Every sound has a **life**: how it's born (attack), how it settles (decay + sustain),
how it says goodbye (release). Sculpt one:

<Toy id="tone-forge" />

Missions:
- Make a **pluck** (born instantly, dies fast) — then make a **foghorn** (slow birth, never leaves).
- Turn up **dirt** and watch square 1 come back: distortion is just *extra helpers*.
- Slide **harmonicity** off a whole number — hear the sound turn bell-clangy. That's rest-or-lean
  happening *inside* a single note.

## Now pluck a real(ish) string

The Forge builds sounds from knobs. Nature builds them from **physics**:

<Toy id="pluck-string" size="inline" />

Where you pluck decides which helpers ring — that's why picking near the bridge sounds
twangy and over the neck sounds round. And touching at 1/2 while plucking? That's the
bell-like *harmonic* guitarists show off with. Go try it on the real guitar right now.
