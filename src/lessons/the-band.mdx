# 🎵 The Band — 12-Bar Blues

The sky at the top of the court. The 12-bar blues is the world's friendliest song form:
three chords, twelve bars, and the moody Five sounds *great* on top of all of them.

Solo on top — A minor flavor, guitar surface (this is why guitarists love open strings):

<Toy id="drone-pad" dials={{ drone: false, scale: "five-minor", home: "57", surface: "frets", stickers: false }} />

Jobs: ground · soloist · pulse (grab a Pulse Grid from the Toybox) · singer.
Trade every 12 bars. **Rules of the band:** mistakes are just leans — resolve them;
nobody stops; land Home together on the last bar.

When you've jammed it, take your riff into the <Link to="/daw">Sketchbook</Link> and
build it into a piece you can keep, version, and hand to someone else to remix.
