# 2 · Rest or Lean

Two sounds can **rest** together, or **lean** on each other and wobble.
Your ears already know the difference — find it with your hands.

## Why does the wobble die?

When it rests, the two waves have found a **simple relationship** — like 3:2, a **leap**.

**The big idea of the whole court:** simple relationships rest; complicated ones lean.
Music is the game of choosing when to do which.
