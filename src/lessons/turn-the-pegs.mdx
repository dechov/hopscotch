# 8 · Turn the Pegs *(regroup)*

Square 4, but for real: **no meter**. This is exactly what tuning a guitar is.

<Toy id="tune-it" mode="guided" guide={pegsByEar} dials={{ meter: false }} />

## Now the real instrument

Grab the guitar. Pick ONE string. **Loosen it a little** (never tighten — strings forgive
loose, not tight). Now play it together with its neighbor and turn the peg until the wobble
slows… slows… stops. You just tuned by ear. The meter app can retire.
