# 15 · Lean the Time

Square 2 taught you that *pitch* can rest or lean. Here's the twist: **time can lean too.**

<Toy id="pulse-grid" dials={{ swing: 0 }} />

Fill in a simple pattern, then slowly raise the **lean** knob while it loops.
The boxes never change — but the groove wakes up. That's **swing**.

Same idea, different knob — a sound can lean as well:

<Toy id="tone-forge" size="inline" dials={{ dirt: 0.35, wobbleDepth: 0.15 }} />

Straight vs. swung, clean vs. dirty, still vs. wobbly. None of them is "right."
They're **flavors of lean**, and now you own the knobs.
