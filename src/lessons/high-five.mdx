# 6 · The Five-Tone Scale

Five notes. One for each finger. And a secret: **nothing in here can clash**.

<Toy id="drone-pad" mode="guided" guide={blackKeysIntro} />

Why can't it clash? Remember square 2: the wobble lives in *crowded* relationships —
notes a **scoot** apart. The five-tone scale has **no scoots in it anywhere**. Every pair of
notes is at least a step apart. It's a playground with no sharp corners.

On a real piano, the five black keys are exactly this. Go find them.
